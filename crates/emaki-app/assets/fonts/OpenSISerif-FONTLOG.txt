OpenSI Serif

Current change (0.394)
- Set the copyright holder to The OpenSI Project Authors and the designer to auraunii.
- Declared "OpenSI" as a Reserved Font Name under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  Outlines, metrics, and styles are unchanged.

Earlier changes
- Renamed the family to OpenSI Serif. Outlines, metrics, and styles were retained.

Earlier glyph changes
- Replaced the lowercase g (and gcircumflex, gbreve, gdotaccent, gcommaaccent/uni0123) in Regular and Semibold
  with the g from Source Serif 4 (SIL OFL 1.1, https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif).
  Modifications: taken from a variable-font instance whose weight matches this font's 'o' stem
  (Regular ~97 units, Semibold ~142), scaled to this font's x-height, and the lower loop lifted
  so the descender aligns with j/p/y (neck shortened, loop shape unchanged). Accents re-centred.
- The family is therefore distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt).
- Italic: unchanged apart from names/version/license fields.
